Hanterill is a desktop app that plugs into your car with one cable and reads what the car's
computers already know. There is no account to create, no server to reach, and nothing you read
leaves your machine.

You do not need to understand the protocol to use it. This page gets you connected. The rest of
these docs explain what each screen shows and where the numbers come from.

## What you need

- A supported car. Polestar 2 and the CMA Volvos (XC40/EX40, C40/EC40) are the tested platforms.
  See [Supported vehicles](/docs/supported-vehicles).
- An ENET cable: OBD-II on one end, RJ45 on the other. It is a plain copper cable with no chip
  inside, so there is no driver to install.
- An Ethernet port on your computer. A USB-C to Ethernet adapter works.
- Hanterill installed. See [Install on your computer](/docs/installation).

## Your first session

1. Turn the car to ignition-on. You do not need to start it.
2. Plug the ENET cable into the car's OBD-II port and the RJ45 end into your computer.
3. Open Hanterill. It looks for a car on the cable and reports what it finds in the title bar.
4. When the state reads **Connected**, open **Overview**. Hanterill reads the car's identity and a
   battery summary in one pass.
5. Go where you need to from there: **Fault codes** for a scan, **Battery** for the high-voltage
   view, **Inspection report** for the scored summary.
6. Sessions archive themselves locally as you work. The **Sessions** screen reopens them later and
   compares any two.

Everything above is a read. Hanterill asks questions and writes nothing back to the car.

## No car handy?

The released app connects to real cars only. Development builds can simulate a car for testing,
and this website has a [simulated scan](/features/vehicle-diagnostics) you can watch, but the app
you download expects a vehicle on the other end of the cable.

For a job-by-job path through the app, use [Common tasks](/docs/common-tasks).

## Where your data goes

Sessions, reads and exports are written to your disk. Nothing is uploaded. See
[Privacy](/docs/privacy).

## If something does not work

If the car never appears, the usual causes are the cable, the adapter, or a firewall. The
[Connection](/docs/connection) page walks through them in order. If a reading looks wrong, read
[Safety](/docs/safety) before acting on it.

[Get help](/docs/get-help) explains how to export a scrubbed support bundle and report a problem.
