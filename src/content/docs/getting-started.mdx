openCMA is a desktop application. It runs on your computer and talks to the vehicle over Ethernet. There is no account to create and nothing to configure in the cloud.

## Before you start

You need:

- A supported vehicle. Polestar 2 and the CMA Volvos (XC40/EX40, C40/EC40) are the tested platforms. See [Supported vehicles](/docs/supported-vehicles).
- A passive ENET cable (OBD-II to RJ45).
- An Ethernet interface on your computer. A USB-C to Ethernet adapter is fine.
- openCMA installed. See [Download](/download).

## First session

1. Turn the vehicle to ignition-on. You do not need to start it.
2. Connect the ENET cable to the OBD-II port and the RJ45 end to your Ethernet interface.
3. Open openCMA. It looks for a vehicle on the link and shows a connection state in the title bar.
4. When the state reads **Connected**, open **Overview**. openCMA reads vehicle identification and a battery summary.
5. Open **DTC** to run a scan, or **Battery** for the full high-voltage view.

Everything so far is read-only. Nothing has been written to the vehicle.

## Where data goes

Sessions, reads and exports are written to your disk. openCMA does not upload anything. See [Privacy](/docs/privacy).
