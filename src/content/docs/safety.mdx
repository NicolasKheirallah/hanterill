openCMA separates reading from changing. Most of what it does is reads. The parts that change the vehicle are grouped, labelled, and off by default.

## Read-only

Battery data, ECU identification, fault codes and live telemetry are reads. Running them cannot change vehicle configuration.

## Operations that change the vehicle

- Clearing diagnostic information (`0x14`)
- Writing data by identifier (`0x2E`)
- Running routines (`0x31`)
- Input/output control (`0x2F`)

<SafetyNotice level="adaptation" title="Off by default">
In openCMA these require enabling write access for the session. They fail closed: if the opt-in is not present, the request is not sent. There is no hidden path around the gate.
</SafetyNotice>

## High voltage

<SafetyNotice level="high-voltage" title="High-voltage systems are hazardous">
Electric vehicles carry hazardous high-voltage systems. Reading battery data over a diagnostic link does not de-energise anything and does not make physical high-voltage service safe. HV service requires the manufacturer's procedures, equipment and training.
</SafetyNotice>

## Not a replacement for manufacturer tooling

openCMA is independent and is not affiliated with Volvo Cars, Polestar or Geely. Do not make a safety-critical repair decision on openCMA output alone, especially on Experimental or Research platforms where decoding is not verified.

## If something looks wrong

Stop. Do not run routines to "fix" a reading you do not understand. Export the session and compare it against a known-good reference or ask in the project's discussions before acting.
