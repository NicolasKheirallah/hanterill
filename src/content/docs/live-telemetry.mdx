A scan shows what the car reported at one point in time. **Live Telemetry** follows selected readings as they change. You can watch them during a test and save the recording for later review.

## 1. Choosing what to watch (Channel presets and custom channels)

At the top of the **Live Telemetry** screen, you can start quickly with built-in channel groups or pick individual measurements from any module:

- **Battery & High Voltage preset**: Pack voltage, pack current, real-time power (kW), State of Charge, and minimum/maximum pack temperatures.
- **Drivetrain & Inverters preset**: Front and rear motor speed, torque delivery, and inverter coolant and stator temperatures.
- **Thermal & Heat Pump preset**: Cabin heater, chiller, radiator, and compressor temperatures and pressures.
- **12 V Low-Voltage preset**: DC-DC converter output voltage, 12 V battery current, and 12 V State of Charge.
- **Custom selection**: Toggle any readable parameter from the catalogue to build your own chart view.

## 2. How live sampling works

When you click **Start live stream**, Hanterill asks the selected control units to sample your chosen channels on a steady schedule:

- **Periodic streaming where supported**: When a module supports periodic streaming, Hanterill registers your chosen channels once and the module streams updates automatically until you pause or leave the screen.
- **Polled sampling fallback**: For channels that do not support periodic streaming, Hanterill polls the module at a steady interval.
- **Why fewer channels update faster**: The car's diagnostic gateway shares its bandwidth across every module you query. Watching 3 to 5 channels gives you a crisp, fast waveform (several updates per second), whereas watching 15 channels spreads the updates across more modules.

## 3. Reading the chart and honest gap accounting

Hanterill plots live data like lab instrumentation rather than a decorative dashboard gauge:

- **Synchronized crosshair**: Move your mouse (or use the Left and Right arrow keys on your keyboard) across the chart to inspect the exact values of every active channel at the same millisecond.
- **Pause and inspect**: Click **Pause** at any moment to freeze the screen and examine a voltage dip or torque spike without stopping the background recording.
- **Honest gap accounting**: If a module is briefly busy and misses a sample interval, Hanterill counts the missed interval and shows a real gap in the line rather than drawing a fake smooth curve across missing data.
- **One stream at a time**: To keep the car's gateway responsive, Hanterill runs only one live stream at a time and automatically stops streaming as soon as you switch to another task.

## 4. Recording a run and replaying it offline

When chasing an intermittent issue (such as a power sag under acceleration or a climate loop that stops cooling after 20 minutes), you can record the entire live session:

1. Select your channels and click **Record**.
2. Drive or run the test (always have a passenger operate the laptop if the car is moving).
3. Click **Stop** when finished.
4. You can **replay the recording offline** inside the app at any time, scrub back and forth along the timeline, or click **Export CSV** to open the time-stamped measurements in Excel, Numbers, or Python.
