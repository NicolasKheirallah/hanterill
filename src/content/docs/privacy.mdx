Hanterill talks to the car through the diagnostic cable and saves sessions on your computer. The desktop app does not require an account or send diagnostic sessions to a cloud service. This guide explains what is stored and what to check before sharing a file.

## What stays on your computer

Everything Hanterill records is stored locally in your user profile folder:

- **Saved sessions, fault scans, and live captures**: Stored in your local session archive together with any notes you write.
- **Exported files**: JSON, CSV, HTML/print reports, and ZIP bundles saved wherever you choose.
- **Application settings**: Your chosen theme, language, audience mode, and connection preferences.
- **Activity log**: A local log of diagnostic operations run during your session, with VINs automatically masked.
- **Per-vehicle discovery cache**: Stored under a one-way hash of the VIN so directory listings on your computer never expose your car's identity.

## What is never collected or transmitted

- No telemetry or background heartbeats
- No analytics or feature usage tracking
- No cloud account, login, or license check
- No vehicle data or fault codes uploaded anywhere
- No third-party crash reporting libraries

The desktop app makes **zero internet connections**. It works completely offline in an underground
garage or on a roadside without Wi-Fi.

## VIN visibility and privacy redaction

Hanterill reads your car's 17-character VIN when connecting so it can identify the vehicle platform,
check for mismatched modules, and keep sessions from two different cars separate. You stay in full
control of when that VIN is shown:

- **On the Vehicle screen and Inspection Reports**: The full 17-character VIN is displayed clearly so you can verify the car against its registration documents and physical VIN plate, with a **one-click privacy toggle** in the toolbar to mask it (`YSM...0428`) whenever you want to take a screenshot or share a report.
- **In other screens**: You can set default VIN masking in **Settings -> Privacy & Storage**.
- **In support bundles**: Exporting a support bundle from **Settings -> About** automatically scrubs VINs, module serial numbers, private IP addresses, and exact GPS coordinates (`[REDACTED-GPS]`) while preserving the diagnostic readings needed to troubleshoot an issue.

## Optional GPS coordinates

Location tagging is disabled by default. If you opt in under **Settings -> Privacy & Storage**, you
can attach approximate coordinates to a session so you remember which workshop or charger a capture
was taken at.

- **Inspection Reports** leave GPS out by default. You can include it when you explicitly enable that option for a report.
- In exports where location is enabled, coordinates may be rounded. Check the location section before sharing a file.

## Storage management, retention, and deletion

In **Settings -> Privacy & Storage**, you can see how much disk space your saved sessions, raw
captures, and logs are using, and choose how long sessions are kept automatically:

- **30 days**, **90 days**, **1 year**, or **Indefinitely**
- **One-click storage cleanup**: Clear cached module discovery records or permanently delete all stored sessions and captures in one step.

<Callout kind="note" title="Deleting local data is permanent">
Because your sessions exist only on your own computer and are never backed up to any cloud server,
deleting sessions from Hanterill removes them permanently unless you exported a copy first.
</Callout>
