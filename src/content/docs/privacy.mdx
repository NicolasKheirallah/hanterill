Hanterill is entirely local. It talks to your car, and it writes to your disk. There is no backend,
no account, and nothing to sign in to.

## What stays on your machine

- Every session, fault read and live capture, kept in a local archive with your notes
- Exports you create: JSON, CSV and ZIP bundles
- App settings, themes and connection preferences
- Activity log: the trail of what was run, with VINs redacted
- Per-car caching, stored under a hash of the VIN so a file listing reveals no identity

## What is never collected

- No telemetry
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No cloud account or backend
- No diagnostic data uploaded anywhere
- No third-party crash reporting

The optional update check is the one internet exception. It is off by default and sends no vehicle
data. See [Network activity](#network-activity).

## VIN and location redaction

The VIN is read during vehicle identification, because a session needs to know which car it belongs
to. Where a full VIN is not needed, it is masked:

- In the interface, the VIN shows as a fragment such as `YSM...0428` by default. You can turn full
  visibility on in Settings.
- In logs and exports, the full VIN is kept only when you explicitly ask for it.
- The support bundle scrubs serial numbers, private addresses, credentials and decimal coordinates
  before a file leaves the machine, as `[REDACTED-GPS]`, while keeping genuine diagnostic readings
  intact.

Location gets the same treatment. Pre-purchase inspection reports carry no GPS sections at all, and
where other exports include coordinates they are rounded to roughly 110 metres.

## Retention and deletion

You choose how long sessions are kept: 30 days, 90 days, a year, or indefinitely. The
Privacy and Storage settings page also has a wipe action that clears cached records and stored
sessions in one step.

<Callout kind="note" title="Deletion means deletion">
Sessions live in local storage on your machine. There is no copy anywhere else, so deleting them
removes them for good.
</Callout>

## Network activity

During a diagnostic session, Hanterill speaks to the car over the local link. It needs no internet
connection to run. In **Settings → Updates**, you can check for a published release now or opt in
to a check on launch, at most once a day. This sends a request to GitHub's public release API;
GitHub sees your IP address, but the request carries no VIN, vehicle data, diagnostic content or
build identity. The app only displays a notice. It never downloads or installs an update.

This website is separate: it reads the GitHub API to show the latest release and repository
statistics. That is the website, not the app.

## No subscription, and no price

There is no paid tier and no license server, and Hanterill is free for private use in every
version: [the license](/docs/license) is where that is written down. The source is available to
read, and Hanterill is licensed for personal, non-commercial use. There is nothing to monetise in
your car's data, so none of it is collected.
