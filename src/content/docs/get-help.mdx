If the car will not connect or a reading looks wrong, start with [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting). If the problem remains, you can save a support bundle for someone to inspect. Review the file yourself before sharing it.

## Before you report an issue

1. Check the [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) checklist first, especially if the car does not appear when you plug in.
2. Check [Supported vehicles](/docs/supported-vehicles) to see whether your vehicle platform is **Supported**, **Partial**, **Testing**, or **Research**. On **Testing** and **Research** vehicles, new module responses and battery layouts are actively being mapped.

## Exporting a privacy-scrubbed Support Bundle

Instead of taking phone photos of your screen or copying logs by hand, you can export a **Support Bundle** directly from the app:

1. Open **Settings -> Support & Diagnostics** (or open the **Activity Log** screen).
2. Click **Export support bundle**.
3. Choose where to save the `.zip` file on your computer.

### What is inside the support bundle

- **Application version and operating system details** so it is clear which release you are running.
- **Recent connection and protocol activity logs** showing which network interfaces were tried, which modules answered, and any error or refusal codes returned by the car.
- **Session metadata and decoder status** for the current session.

### What is automatically removed for your privacy

Before Hanterill writes the support bundle to disk, it automatically scrubs personal and identifying information:

- **Your 17-character VIN** is replaced with a masked placeholder (`REDACTED-VIN`).
- **Module hardware serial numbers** and **Bluetooth / Wi-Fi / Ethernet MAC addresses** are scrubbed.
- **Exact GPS coordinates** (if you queried the satellite screen during the session) are removed.
- **Your computer's local username and home folder paths** are stripped from file paths.

Because the bundle is a normal `.zip` archive saved to your computer, you can open it and inspect its contents before sharing it. Hanterill never uploads support bundles automatically.

## Where to open a report

When you report a problem, include:

- Your vehicle model, model year, and powertrain (for example, *2023 Polestar 2 Long Range Dual Motor* or *2024 Volvo EX30 Single Motor Extended Range*).
- What you were trying to do and what appeared on screen.
- Your exported support bundle (or a saved session export with **Mask VIN** enabled).
