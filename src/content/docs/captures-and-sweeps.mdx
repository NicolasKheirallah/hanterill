The **Battery Health** and **Climate** screens read the values needed for those tasks. **Captures & Sweeps** collects a broader record across responding control units. Use it when you want to keep a detailed snapshot or investigate a value that does not appear on a regular screen.

## 1. What a full-car sweep collects

A full-car sweep walks through the platform's module catalogue (up to 49 modules on CMA) and reads the known data parameters for each module that answers:

- **Module identity and software versions** across every reachable control unit.
- **All catalogued battery, thermal, drivetrain, chassis, body, and telematics parameters**.
- **Response status and timing**: For every parameter requested, Hanterill records whether the module returned a value, politely reported that the parameter is not supported on this car's trim level, or timed out, along with how many milliseconds the module took to reply.

## 2. How a sweep progresses and how to pause or stop it

Because a full sweep queries dozens of modules and hundreds of individual parameters, the **Captures & Sweeps** screen shows live progress module by module:

- **Fast discovery first**: Hanterill checks which modules are fitted to your car so it does not waste time waiting on optional modules your car does not have.
- **Gentle handling of sleeping sub-bus modules**: Some comfort and body modules sit on secondary networks that nap even while the dashboard is awake. Hanterill uses a safe wake-up sequence when probing quiet modules and allows them to return to sleep afterward.
- **Stop or cancel at any time**: Clicking **Stop sweep** cancels the remaining queue immediately and keeps every reading collected up to that point.

## 3. Resuming an interrupted sweep (Retry missed modules)

If a door closes, the car's gateway briefly pauses, or a busy module times out halfway through a sweep, you never have to start over from scratch:

- Click **Retry missed modules** on the **Captures & Sweeps** screen.
- Hanterill keeps all the modules that already completed and re-queries **only the modules that timed out or were interrupted**, merging their new answers into the same capture.

## 4. Re-checking saved captures offline

Every sweep you run is saved locally on your computer as a complete capture file:

- **Offline parameter browsing**: You can open any saved capture in the **DID Explorer** indoors, without being connected to the car, and search or filter every value your car returned.
- **Automatic re-decoding as the catalogue grows**: Because the capture stores the module's exact response alongside the decoded value, whenever a new release of Hanterill adds new parameter definitions or improved decoders, you can open your old saved captures and see the newly decoded values immediately without plugging back into the car.
- **Smart per-car learning**: Hanterill remembers which parameters each module in your car actually supports, making repeat sweeps on the same vehicle faster.
