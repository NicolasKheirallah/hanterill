The high-voltage battery data comes from the Battery Energy Control Module (BECM) over UDS. openCMA opens an extended session with the BECM and reads by identifier.

## What you get

- State of Health and State of Charge
- Pack voltage and pack current
- All 108 cell-group potentials on CMA: 27 modules, 4 groups each
- Minimum, maximum and delta across cell groups
- Pack and module temperatures
- Estimated usable capacity against nominal
- BECM hardware and software part numbers

## Reading it

After routing activation:

```text
0x10 03            enter extended diagnostic session (BECM)
0x22 49 6D         read battery state of health
0x22 16 35         read pack voltage
0x22 <cell block>  read cell-group potentials
0x19 02 FF         read DTC by status mask
```

Values are decoded against the CMA battery layout. The cell-group potentials are read as a block and mapped to module and group positions, not derived from a single summary field.

## Reading the cell matrix

A healthy pack sits within a few millivolts across all 108 groups. A widening delta, or one module consistently offset from the pack mean, is more informative than the SOH percentage alone. The website's [battery section](/features/battery-health) shows the same matrix with sample data.

## Provisional platforms

<Callout kind="warning" title="Unverified on SEA and SPA2">
On SEA and SPA2 the BECM identifiers and scaling differ and are not verified. openCMA marks battery data on those platforms as unverified and it should not be used for decisions.
</Callout>
