A **session** is a saved visit to a vehicle. In **Sessions & Reports**, you can add a note, reopen the readings captured during that visit and export them later. What a session contains depends on which screens and scans you actually ran before saving it.

## Save and review a visit

Connect to the car, collect the readings you need and choose **Save current session**. A battery report, fault scan, firmware audit or recording appears in the session only if it was captured. Missing work is not silently filled in. The session list remains available when the car is disconnected.

If a scan stopped early, check its coverage before treating the saved session as complete. A module that was not read during the visit cannot become a verified result simply because the session was saved.

## Compare two visits

Select two sessions to open the fault-code comparison. It shows which recorded codes changed between the selected visits. The battery page also has a trend for SoH, SoC and cell-group SoH spread from saved battery reports. That trend is different from the Cell Map's voltage spread.

Other comparison views can show changes in firmware or module inventory when both captures contain the required data. **Compare cell captures** needs two cell captures supplied to that tool; selecting two sessions does not automatically compare every cell voltage. Always confirm that both visits belong to the same car.

## Since your last visit

When you connect, Hanterill saves a small set of readings for later comparison. It includes lifetime charge counts, time in each charge band, selected 12 V statistics, the gateway reset log, clocks from two modules and the VIN. The capture takes seconds; it does not start a full scan.

The capture is compared with the previous stored one for the same car, and the **Since your last visit** list on the Sessions screen reports what actually changed: a charge count that grew, a reset counter that advanced, clocks that lost sync, a key that was added, fault codes that were cleared, or new charger alarms. Findings also appear as toasts when you connect. The first capture stores a baseline and says so; a capture from a different car is never diffed against this one. The trend view shows one row per saved session with the deltas chained per vehicle, so repeated visits build a history without you running anything manually.

## Export and privacy

Sessions can be exported as JSON, as CSV tables and as collections of files. You can import a supported session file on another computer. Check VIN masking before sharing: an export may contain vehicle identity and detailed readings. A support bundle is a separate, privacy-filtered export for reporting a problem.

Keep the original saved data when comparing visits or rescoring an older report. A later version of the app may interpret the same captured readings differently, but it cannot add a measurement that was never taken.
