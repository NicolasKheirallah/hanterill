Hanterill saves each scan as a **session** on your computer. In **Sessions & Reports**, you can review past readings, add notes, compare two visits and export a record. The history remains available when the car is disconnected.

## 1. What is saved inside a session

A saved session is a complete, time-stamped record of what your car reported during that visit:

- **Vehicle identity and mileage**: Platform, model year, powertrain, odometer readings across modules, and VIN (which you can mask with one click whenever sharing or exporting).
- **High-voltage battery and 12 V health**: State of Health, State of Charge, pack voltage and current, all individual cell-group voltages, and thermal readings.
- **Fault codes and freeze-frame snapshots**: Every fault code reported across the car, its live status (*Active*, *Pending*, *Stored*, *Historical*), and the freeze-frame conditions saved when the fault tripped.
- **Module inventory and software versions**: Every control unit that answered, along with its hardware and software part numbers.
- **Subsystem readings and raw module responses**: All decoded subsystem measurements plus the underlying raw module responses, so you can filter, inspect, or re-evaluate a saved session offline at any time without plugging back into the car.
- **Your operator notes**: Free-text notes you attach to the session (for example, *"After replacing 12 V battery"* or *"Pre-purchase check on silver 2022 Dual Motor"*).

### Safe local storage
Sessions are written atomically to your computer's local application data folder so a power loss or sudden unplugging never corrupts your archive. If a file on disk is ever damaged, Hanterill quarantined and recovers it automatically on startup.

## 2. The five built-in comparisons

Having two saved sessions lets you prove what changed over time or after a workshop visit:

1. **Fault Code Diff (DTC Diff)**: Pick a "before repair" and "after repair" session to see which fault codes were **cleared**, which **remained**, which **newly appeared**, and which changed status (for example, from *Active* to *Stored*).
2. **Battery Health History & Trend**: Tracks a single car's **State of Health (SoH)**, **State of Charge (SoC)**, and **cell spread (mV)** across every saved visit over months and years, with one-click CSV export.
3. **Cell-by-Cell History Diff**: Compares every individual cell group (all 108 positions on a CMA pack, or the matching count on SPA and SEA packs) between two sessions to see whether a weak cell group recovered after balancing or drifted further.
4. **Module Inventory Diff**: Shows if a control unit went missing on the network or started answering again after a wiring or fuse repair.
5. **Firmware & Software Diff**: Compares every module's software and hardware part numbers between two sessions to show the exact list of modules updated by an over-the-air (OTA) update or a dealer service visit.

## 3. Exporting and sharing your data

You own everything Hanterill reads from your car. From **Sessions & Reports**, you can export any session in standard formats:

| Export format | What it contains | Best used for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Printable PDF / Print view** | Formatted **Executive inspection**, **Battery certificate**, or **Technical dossier** report. | Handing to a buyer, seller, or workshop, or keeping in your car's service folder. |
| **JSON session export** | The complete structured session record, including decoded measurements and raw module responses (with optional VIN masking). | Archiving, backing up, or importing into another computer running Hanterill. |
| **CSV spreadsheets** | Tables for cell-group voltages, fault codes, module software versions, live telemetry recordings, and battery health trends. | Opening in Excel, Numbers, Google Sheets, or data-analysis scripts. |
| **Support bundle (`.zip`)** | Privacy-scrubbed diagnostic logs and session summary with VIN, serial numbers, and GPS automatically removed. | Attaching to a bug report or support question (see [Get help](/docs/get-help)). |
