The **12 V Sleep Drain** screen helps you see whether the car reports unusually high current use while parked. It reads a current estimate and a distribution of stored current samples from the car's central electronics module. It does not identify which part of the car woke up.

## Why an awake reading is different

Opening a door or connecting diagnostic equipment wakes parts of the car. Current measured at that moment is not the same as current while it sleeps. The drain screen uses the car's stored sample distribution to give you more context than one live reading.

Choose **Read 12V drain** to see the reported average current and how many samples fell into each current range. The app also compares the average with the distribution. If they disagree substantially, it marks the result as inconsistent instead of quietly combining them.

## How to use the result

A larger share of samples in higher-current ranges can suggest that the car spent more time awake or drawing power. It does not name the responsible module or explain when each sample was taken. The screen has no wake-event timeline, 12 V battery health reading or total energy used while parked. You can export the displayed ranges as CSV.

If the 12 V battery goes flat, compare drain readings taken under similar conditions and check the battery's condition separately. Charging, remote services and ordinary scheduled activity can all affect what the car does while parked. A drain result is evidence to investigate, not proof that a particular component is faulty.
