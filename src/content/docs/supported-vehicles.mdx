Hanterill is built CMA-first. The Compact Modular Architecture platform under Polestar 2 and the smaller electric Volvos is where battery, ECU and routine coverage was written and validated.

## Platform compatibility is not support

Two different things:

- **Platform compatibility** means Hanterill can reach the vehicle over DoIP.
- **Verified support** means the diagnostic functions are implemented and checked
  against known-good references.

A car can be reachable and still expose almost nothing useful. Status labels on
this site always reflect verified support.

## Status labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Supported | Verified runtime implementation. Battery, DTC, ECU identification and live data are exercised regularly. |
| Partial | Some functions verified, others not yet covered on this vehicle. |
| Testing | Implementation exists but needs validation. |
| WIP | Actively being developed. Connects, but decoding is incomplete. |
| Research | Architecture being investigated. Not usable for diagnostics. |
| Planned | On the roadmap. No implementation yet. |

### SPA

<Callout kind="caution" title="Not verified on a live vehicle">
The SPA battery catalogue ships: pack topologies for the 69 kWh and 78 kWh EV packs and the
102-cell hybrid, the CAN-era BECM registry and its decoders, ported from community research with
cited provenance. Nothing is verified against a live vehicle yet, and on first-generation SPA the
battery answers over CAN-era plumbing, not today's DoIP path. Do not rely on figures from these
cars.
</Callout>

<PlatformSupport platform="SPA" />

### SEA

<Callout kind="caution" title="Hardware confirmation pending">
SEA1 is defined in the catalogue: ECU registry, DoIP routing addresses, DID entries and battery
cell mapping for EX30-class cars, with 107S and 110S topologies. Every definition is
hardware-unconfirmed, held up only by synthetic fixtures. Zeekr and other sub-platforms
deliberately do not inherit the EX30 map until their own mapping is confirmed.
</Callout>

## Pack topologies

The cell matrix takes its topology from the catalogue, never a hard-coded constant:

| Platform | Topologies | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CMA | 108S: 27 modules of 4 groups | Verified on the reference vehicle |
| SPA | EV 96S (69 kWh), EV 108S (78 kWh), hybrid 102S | Catalogued, hardware verification pending |
| SEA1 | 107S, 110S | Catalogued, hardware verification pending |

A pack reports complete at its own cell count: a 102-cell hybrid is never judged against 108.
