The **Charging** screen helps you investigate a charge session that is slow or will not start. It shows the charging readings the car makes available and offers a separate **Diagnose charge limit** check. Read the result alongside the charger display and any fault codes; one missing value cannot identify the cause.

## What the screen reads

Choose **Read charging telemetry** to see the charge-port lock state, the cable's reported current rating and the battery pack voltage. A blank value means that reading was not returned. The screen does not show a complete AC or DC charge curve.

AC charging uses the car's on-board charger to turn power from a wallbox into power the battery can store. At a DC fast charger, the external station supplies DC power. In either case, the available charging power can change as the battery warms or fills.

## Diagnose a charging limit

**Diagnose charge limit** is a separate read. It uses the available cable rating, the car's charge-power limit, battery temperature, charge level, actual power and whether the car reports that it is charging. Depending on those inputs, the app may point to cable capability, the on-board charger, battery temperature or charge level. If the inputs do not support a conclusion, it shows an unknown result.

A limit shown during one scan describes the car's state at that moment. For example, a high charge level can reduce charging power, but the reading does not prove that the charger is working correctly. If charging will not start, check whether the plug is seated and locked, then compare the car's message with the station's message and scan for fault codes.

## When a reading is missing

The app shows whether each charging value was measured or unavailable. Do not treat a blank cable rating, lock state or power value as zero. Repeat the read while the car is connected to the charger if the first attempt did not return enough information.
